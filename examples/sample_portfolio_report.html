Limits

The risk score is a monitoring aid built from the records supplied and a set of illustrative thresholds and weights. It is not a credit rating and says nothing certain about any supplier's solvency. The incident probability is an estimate from past patterns: among suppliers given 30%, about three in ten are expected to have an incident in the following quarter. Thresholds, weights and the regional hazard index are illustrative settings, not measurements. With a supplier base of this size the model's accuracy figures rest on a small number of incidents and will move when it is refitted.
