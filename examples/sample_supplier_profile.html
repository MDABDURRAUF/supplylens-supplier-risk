How to read this

The risk score is a monitoring aid built from the records supplied and a set of illustrative thresholds and weights. It is not a credit rating and says nothing certain about any supplier's solvency. The incident probability is an estimate from past patterns: among suppliers given 30%, about three in ten are expected to have an incident in the following quarter.
